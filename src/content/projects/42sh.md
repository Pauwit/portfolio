A four-person EPITA systems assignment (January 2025): a POSIX-ish shell in C, with a hand-rolled lexer and recursive-descent parser rather than a parser generator. It covers builtins, variable and glob expansion, pipes, redirections, and job control, all executed off an AST rather than direct interpretation.

Built with Autotools under strict compiler flags (`-Wall -Wextra -Werror -Wvla -pedantic -std=c99`). The motivation was straightforward: understand exactly what a shell is doing between a keystroke and a running process, instead of just using one every day.
