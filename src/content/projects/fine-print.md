Built for SME credit teams: ingest a loan agreement PDF, run every covenant clause through an AI model (Cerebras/Mistral) against 21 rules across six categories, Solvency, Liquidity, Profitability, Structural, Portfolio, and KYC/AML. Results render as an interactive knowledge graph, agreement at the center, category hubs branching out, individual covenant nodes color-coded by compliance status.

A clause inspector shows the extracted quote and the model's commentary per node, and analysts can accept or override findings before exporting a one-click compliance report. Team project at the Paris Fintech Hackathon 2026.
